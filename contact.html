Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding admissions, batch sizes, and trials? Here are quick answers.

Is there a trial session before admission?

Yes! Every prospective student receives one free diagnostic fitness and skills assessment session with our head coaches before finalizing admission.

What is the minimum age for enrollment?

We accept kids starting from 4.5 years old for grassroots development (such as introductory swimming and basic football motor skills) up to adult fitness enthusiasts.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

We provide all training balls, nets, markers, and training props. Students bring their own personal sports gear (like cricket bats/pads, tennis rackets, or swimsuits).

Can students switch sports batches?

Yes, batch timing adjustments and dual-sport enrollments (e.g. Football in morning + Swimming in evening) can be coordinated with our admissions desk.
